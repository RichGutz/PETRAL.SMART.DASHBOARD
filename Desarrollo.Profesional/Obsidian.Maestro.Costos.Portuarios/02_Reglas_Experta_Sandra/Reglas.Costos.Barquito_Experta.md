## 📐 1. Fórmulas de Cálculo por Ítem

### A) SHIFTING EXPENSES
1. **Practicaje (`Pilotage`)**:
   $$\text{Pilotage USD} = \text{Tarifa Base Autoridad Marítima Chile} \times \text{GRT}$$
   - *BT MOQUEGUA (8259 GRT)*: $\$1,151.01 \text{ USD}$
   - *BT TABLONES (11365 GRT)*: $\$1,388.62 \text{ USD}$
   - *BT HUEMUL (13666 GRT)*: $\$1,609.80 \text{ USD}$
   - *CONCON TRADER (11773 GRT)*: $\$1,540.77 \text{ USD}$

2. **Remolcaje (`Towage Ultratug`)**:
   $$\text{Towage USD} = \$6,776.25 \text{ USD} \times 5 = \$33,881.25 \text{ USD Flat}$$

3. **Seguro de Práctico & Linesmen**:
   - *Pilot Insurance*: $\$110.00 \times 3 = \$330.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Linesmen*: $\$1,500.00 \times 2 = \$3,000.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Port Toll / Access*: $\$90.00 \text{ USD}$

---

### B) GENERAL PORT EXPENSES
4. **Derechos de Faro y Balisas (`Light Dues Chile`)**:
   - **Regla Por Viaje (Spot / Huemul & Concon Trader)**:
     $$\text{Light Dues USD} = \$1.60 \text{ USD} \times \text{GRT}$$
     - *Huemul*: $1.60 \times 13,666 = \$21,865.60 \text{ USD}$
     - *Concon Trader*: $1.60 \times 11,773 = \$18,836.80 \text{ USD}$
   - **Regla Anual Prorrateada (Moquegua & Tablones)**:
     - *Moquegua*: $\$2,240.94 \text{ USD}$
     - *Tablones*: $\$4,465.06 \text{ USD}$

5. **Muellaje Terminal Barquito (`Dockage`)**:
   $$\text{Dockage USD} = \$71.92 \text{ USD/h} \times \text{Horas Puerto (hrs)}$$
   - *Moquegua / Tablones (32h)*: $71.92 \times 32 = \$2,301.44 \text{ USD}$
   - *Huemul / Concon Trader (40h)*: $71.92 \times 40 = \$2,876.80 \text{ USD}$

6. **Remolcador Stand-By Exigido por Autoridad (`Tugboat Stand By`)**:
   $$\text{Tugboat Stand-By USD} = \$650.00 \text{ USD/h} \times \text{Horas Puerto (hrs)}$$
   - *Moquegua / Tablones (32h)*: $650.00 \times 32 = \$20,800.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Huemul / Concon Trader (40h)*: $650.00 \times 40 = \$26,000.00 \text{ USD}$

7. **Lancha Stand-By Operativa (`Launch Stand By`)**:
   $$\text{Launch Stand-By USD} = \$110.00 \text{ USD/h} \times \text{Horas Puerto (hrs)}$$
   - *Moquegua / Tablones (32h)*: $110.00 \times 32 = \$3,520.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Huemul / Concon Trader (40h)*: $110.00 \times 40 = \$4,400.00 \text{ USD}$

8. **Navegación Remolcador desde Caldera (`Tugboat Navigation`)**:
   $$\text{Tugboat Navigation USD} = \$750.00 \text{ USD} \times 6 = \$4,500.00 \text{ USD Flat}$$

9. **Otras Lanchas & Transportes**:
   - *Launch Mooring*: $\$720.00 \times 4 = \$2,880.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Anchorage*: $\$420.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Clearances*: $\$420.00 \times 2 = \$840.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Pilot Transport*: $\$165.00 \times 2 = \$330.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Linesmen Transport*: $\$450.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Authorities Transport & Charges*: $\$750.00 + \$700.00 = \$1,450.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Immigration, Health & Loading Master*: $\$28 + \$130 + \$2,450 = \$2,608.00 \text{ USD}$
