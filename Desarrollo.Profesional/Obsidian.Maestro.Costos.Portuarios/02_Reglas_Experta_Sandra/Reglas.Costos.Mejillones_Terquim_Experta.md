## 📐 1. Fórmulas de Cálculo por Ítem

### A) SHIFTING EXPENSES
1. **Practicaje (`Pilotage`)**:
   $$\text{Pilotage USD} = \text{Tarifa Base Autoridad Marítima Chile} \times \text{GRT}$$
   - *BT MOQUEGUA (8259 GRT)*: $\$1,156.26 \text{ USD}$
   - *BT TABLONES (11365 GRT)*: $\$1,591.10 \text{ USD}$
   - *BT HUEMUL (13666 GRT)*: $\$1,609.80 \text{ USD}$
   - *CONCON TRADER (11773 GRT)*: $\$1,540.77 \text{ USD}$

2. **Remolcaje (`Towage Ultratug`)**:
   - *BT MOQUEGUA*: $\$2,800.00 \times 3 = \$8,400.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Otros Buques*: $\$2,800.00 \times 4 = \$11,200.00 \text{ USD}$

3. **Seguro de Práctico (`Pilot Insurance`)**:
   - *MOQUEGUA & TABLONES*: $\$110.00 \times 2 = \$220.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *HUEMUL & CONCON TRADER*: $\$110.00 \times 3 = \$330.00 \text{ USD}$

4. **Amarradores en Tierra (`Linesmen`)**:
   $$\text{Linesmen USD} = \$801.00 \text{ USD} \times 2 = \$1,602.00 \text{ USD Flat}$$

### B) GENERAL PORT EXPENSES
5. **Derechos de Faro y Balisas (`Light Dues Chile`)**:
   - **Regla Por Viaje (Spot / Huemul & Concon Trader)**:
     $$\text{Light Dues USD} = \$1.60 \text{ USD} \times \text{GRT}$$
     - *Huemul*: $1.60 \times 13,666 = \$21,865.60 \text{ USD}$
     - *Concon Trader*: $1.60 \times 11,773 = \$18,836.80 \text{ USD}$
   - **Regla Anual Prorrateada (Moquegua & Tablones)**:
     $$\text{Light Dues USD} = \frac{\$4.07 \text{ USD} \times \text{GRT}}{15 \text{ viajes}}$$
     - *Moquegua*: $(4.07 \times 8,259) / 15 = \$2,240.94 \text{ USD}$
     - *Tablones*: $(4.07 \times 11,365) / 15 = \$3,083.70 \text{ USD}$

6. **Muellaje Terminal Terquim (`Dockage Terquim`)**:
   $$\text{Dockage USD} = \$5.72 \text{ USD} \times \text{LOA (m)} \times \text{Horas Puerto (hrs)}$$
   - *Moquegua (30h)*: $5.72 \times 134.16 \times 30 = \$23,021.86 \text{ USD}$
   - *Tablones (30h)*: $5.72 \times 158.80 \times 30 = \$27,250.08 \text{ USD}$
   - *Huemul (30h)*: $5.72 \times 161.12 \times 30 = \$27,648.19 \text{ USD}$
   - *Concon Trader (30h)*: $5.72 \times 145.53 \times 30 = \$24,972.95 \text{ USD}$

7. **Lanchas Operativas, Coordinación & Clearance**:
   - *Launch Mooring*: $\$450.00 \times 4 = \$1,800.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Embarcadero*: $\$280.00 \text{ USD}$ (ó $\$420.00 \text{ USD}$)
   - *Launch Anchorage*: $\$390.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Clearances*: $\$420.00 \times 2 = \$840.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Pier Usage*: $\$420.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Pilot Transport*: $\$165.00 \times 2 = \$330.00 \text{ USD}$ (ó $\$495.00 \text{ USD}$)
   - *Authorities Transport*: $\$650.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Authorities Charges*: $\$700.00 \text{ USD}$ Moquegua / $\$0.00$
   - *ISPS Fee*: $\$1,191.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Immigration & Health*: $\$28.00 + \$120.00 = \$148.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Loading Master*: $\$2,923.00 \text{ USD}$ (ó $\$3,108.29 \text{ USD}$)
