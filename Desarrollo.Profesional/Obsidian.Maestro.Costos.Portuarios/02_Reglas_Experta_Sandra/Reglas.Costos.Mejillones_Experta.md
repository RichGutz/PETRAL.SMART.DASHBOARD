> **Terminal Portuario**: TPM Mejillones (Terminal Puerto Mejillones S.A. - Chile) 🇨🇱

---

## 📐 1. Fórmulas de Cálculo por Ítem

### A) SHIFTING EXPENSES
1. **Practicaje (`Pilotage`)**:
   $$\text{Pilotage USD} = \text{Tarifa Base Autoridad Marítima Chile} \times \text{GRT}$$
   - *Valores de la Experta*: BT MOQUEGUA ($1,207.38 USD), BT TABLONES ($1,591.10 USD), BT HUEMUL ($1,609.80 USD), CONCON TRADER ($1,540.77 USD).

2. **Remolcaje (`Towage Ultratug`)**:
   $$\text{Towage USD} = \$2,800.00 \text{ USD/remolcador} \times 4 \text{ maniobras/remolques} = \$11,200.00 \text{ USD Flat}$$

3. **Amarradores en Tierra (`Linesmen`)**:
   $$\text{Linesmen USD} = \$871.25 \text{ USD} \times 2 = \$1,742.50 \text{ USD Flat}$$

---

### B) GENERAL PORT EXPENSES
4. **Derechos de Faro y Balisas (`Light Dues Chile`)**:
   - **Regla Por Viaje (Spot / Huemul & Concon Trader)**:
     $$\text{Light Dues USD} = \$1.60 \text{ USD} \times \text{GRT}$$
     - *Huemul*: $1.60 \times 13,666 = \$21,865.60 \text{ USD}$
     - *Concon Trader*: $1.60 \times 11,773 = \$18,836.80 \text{ USD}$
   - **Regla Anual Prorrateada (Moquegua & Tablones)**:
     $$\text{Light Dues USD} = \frac{\$4.07 \text{ USD} \times \text{GRT}}{15 \text{ viajes}}$$
     - *Moquegua*: $(4.07 \times 8,259) / 15 = \$2,240.94 \text{ USD}$
     - *Tablones*: $(4.07 \times 11,365) / 15 = \$3,083.70 \text{ USD}$

5. **Muellaje Puerto Mejillones (`Dockage`)**:
   $$\text{Dockage USD} = \$3.99 \text{ USD} \times \text{LOA (m)} \times \text{Horas Puerto (hrs)}$$
   - *Moquegua (36h)*: $3.99 \times 134.16 \times 36 = \$19,270.74 \text{ USD}$
   - *Tablones (36h)*: $3.99 \times 158.80 \times 36 = \$22,810.03 \text{ USD}$
   - *Huemul (36h)*: $3.99 \times 161.12 \times 36 = \$23,143.28 \text{ USD}$
   - *Concon Trader (36h)*: $3.99 \times 145.53 \times 36 = \$20,903.93 \text{ USD}$

6. **Lanchas Operativas, Coordinación & Clearance**:
   - *Launch Anchorage*: $\$390.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Pier usage*: $\$272.57 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Mooring/Unmooring*: $\$450.00 \times 4 = \$1,800.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Launch Clearances*: $\$420.00 \text{ USD}$ (ó $\$840.00 \text{ USD}$)
   - *Pilot Transport*: $\$280.00 \text{ USD}$ a $\$560.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Pilot Insurance*: $\$330.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Authorities Transport*: $\$650.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *ISPS Fee*: $\$1,140.35 \text{ USD}$
   - *Immigration & Health*: $\$29.00 + \$110.00 = \$139.00 \text{ USD}$
   - *Loading Master*: $\$3,264.40 \text{ USD}$ ($62 USD/h x 36h + fee)
